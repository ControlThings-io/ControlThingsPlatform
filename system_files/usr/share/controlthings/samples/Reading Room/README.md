# Reading Guide

This collection supports study of industrial control systems (ICS), operational
technology (OT), and cybersecurity. It includes practical guidance, standards,
sector publications, research papers, and editable policy examples.

## Where to start

Choose a reading path for your question or assignment:

- **OT security foundations:** [The Five ICS Cybersecurity Critical Controls - 2022.10](SANS%20Institute/The%20Five%20ICS%20Cybersecurity%20Critical%20Controls%20-%202022.10.pdf). Start with five practical priorities for protecting industrial environments.
- **OT security in depth:** [NIST SP 800-82 - Guide to Operational Technology (OT) Security - Revision 3 - 2023.09](US%20-%20NIST%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Standards%20and%20Technology/SP%20-%20Special%20Publications/NIST%20SP%20800-82%20-%20Guide%20to%20Operational%20Technology%20%28OT%29%20Security%20-%20Revision%203%20-%202023.09.pdf). Use this broader reference for OT architectures, threats, safeguards, and risk.
- **Cybersecurity outcomes:** [NIST - Cybersecurity Framework - v2.0 - 2024.02.26](US%20-%20NIST%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Standards%20and%20Technology/Cybersecurity%20Framework/NIST%20-%20Cybersecurity%20Framework%20-%20v2.0%20-%202024.02.26.pdf). Use the CSF to organize and discuss cybersecurity outcomes.
- **Applying the CSF:** [NIST SP 1301 - Cybersecurity Framework - Quick Start Guide for Creating and Using Organizational Profiles - v2.0 - 2024.02.26](US%20-%20NIST%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Standards%20and%20Technology/SP%20-%20Special%20Publications/NIST%20SP%201301%20-%20Cybersecurity%20Framework%20-%20Quick%20Start%20Guide%20for%20Creating%20and%20Using%20Organizational%20Profiles%20-%20v2.0%20-%202024.02.26.pdf). Create current and target organizational profiles and identify gaps.
- **Finding supporting controls:** [NIST SP 1347 - Cybersecurity Framework - Informative References Quick Start Guide - v2.0 - 2026.08](US%20-%20NIST%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Standards%20and%20Technology/SP%20-%20Special%20Publications/NIST%20SP%201347%20-%20Cybersecurity%20Framework%20-%20Informative%20References%20Quick%20Start%20Guide%20-%20v2.0%20-%202026.08.pdf). Learn to interpret mappings to other standards; use the accompanying workbook.
- **CSF mappings and examples:** [NIST - Cybersecurity Framework - Informative References and Implementation Examples - v2.0 - Undated](US%20-%20NIST%20-%20National%20Institute%20of%20Standards%20and%20Technology/Cybersecurity%20Framework/NIST%20-%20Cybersecurity%20Framework%20-%20Informative%20References%20and%20Implementation%20Examples%20-%20v2.0%20-%20Undated.xlsx). Explore supporting controls and illustrative actions in the Excel workbook.
- **Incident response:** [Dynamic Incident Response - A Framework for Security Teams - Edition 1 - 2026.09.15](SANS%20Institute/Dynamic%20Incident%20Response%20-%20A%20Framework%20for%20Security%20Teams%20-%20Edition%201%20-%202026.09.15.pdf). Study a framework for organizing incident response work.
- **OT asset inventory:** [Foundations for OT Cybersecurity - Asset Inventory Guidance for Owners and Operators - 2025.08.13](US%20-%20CISA%20-%20Cybersecurity%20and%20Infrastructure%20Security%20Agency/Foundations%20for%20OT%20Cybersecurity%20-%20Asset%20Inventory%20Guidance%20for%20Owners%20and%20Operators%20-%202025.08.13.pdf). Identify and document the assets needed to understand an OT environment.

## Finding documents

Folders group documents by publisher or lead issuing organization, with series
subfolders where useful. Government folder prefixes identify AU (Australia),
DE (Germany), GB (United Kingdom), JP (Japan), and US (United States). EU identifies
the European Union. Joint guidance is filed under its lead issuer.

- **CISA, ASD, and NCSC:** OT security principles, asset inventories, connectivity,
  procurement, logging, and practical defensive guidance.
- **NIST:** cybersecurity frameworks, technical guidance, cryptographic standards,
  manufacturing examples, and smart grid reports. SP means Special Publication;
  IR means Interagency or Internal Report; FIPS means Federal Information
  Processing Standard.
- **SANS Institute:** incident response and ICS critical controls.
- **DOE, NERC, and FERC:** energy security, maturity models, reliability standards,
  and regulatory orders.
- **IAEA, NRC, and NEI:** nuclear security guidance, regulations, and industry plans.
- **SEDC:** editable policy and form examples. Adapt these to the scenario;
  they are not a complete security program by themselves.
- **Research Papers, ASAP-SG, and InGuardians:** background research, smart grid
  security profiles, and metering attack methodology.

## Reading editions and status

The date at the end of a filename identifies the publication edition, at the
precision available. `Undated` means no publication date was established; it does
not mean the document is recent. Versions use labels such as `v2.0`; revisions
and editions retain their own labels.

- **CSF workbook:** contains both informative references and implementation
  examples. It is a live export retrieved on October 18, 2026. Mappings identify
  supporting material; they do not demonstrate that an outcome is fulfilled.
  NIST does not endorse non-NIST mappings in its catalog. Consult the
  [NIST resource page](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework/informative-references)
  for refreshed data.
- **SP 800-53:** the Rev 5 PDF includes December 2020 updates. The separate Release
  5.2.0 summary describes later changes; it is not the full updated control catalog.
  Follow the [NIST publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final)
  for control catalog resources.
- **NERC:** `Future Enforcement` contains approved future editions. Distinguish
  these from the editions in the main CIP folder. Check jurisdiction, effective
  dates, and implementation plans in the
  [NERC catalog](https://www.nerc.com/standards/reliability-standards/cip).
- **NEI 08-09:** Rev 7 is under `Submitted Revisions`. Its transmittal requests NRC
  endorsement; submission alone does not establish endorsement.
- **Regulations:** the Part 73 PDF is the annual edition revised January 1, 2026.
  Consult the [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/chapter-I/part-73)
  for subsequent amendments.
- **Historical readings:** CPNI guidance, older CISA/DHS recommended practices,
  NIST manufacturing examples, the smart grid introduction, and SEDC templates
  provide context. Assess their assumptions against the system being studied.
  A newer framework does not automatically update its companion documents.
- **Other editions:** the CSA guidance here is v4; see the
  [publisher's v5 page](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/security-guidance-v5)
  for newer guidance. The Chemical Sector implementation guide here is the 2015
  edition; a May 2020 revision has been identified but is not included.
